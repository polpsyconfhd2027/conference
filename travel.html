Accommodation

We recommend booking your accommodation early after your note of acceptance, as the city of Heidelberg can be quite busy. Below we list some options we can recommend (no affiliation):

  • Atlantic Hotel Heidelberg (Upscale hotel located at Heidelberg main train station)
  • Candlewood Suites (Modern apartments located closest to the conference venue)
  • Hotel Bayrischer Hof (Centrally located, basic, and affordable Hotel)

More option can be found by searching the known booking portals like Booking.com or Airbnb.
