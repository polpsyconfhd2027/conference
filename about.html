General Information

As in previous years, there is no participation fee. The conference theme is Digitalization, Discourse, and Democracy - Political Psychology in the Modern Age. Despite this focus, the conference welcomed and received submissions on all topics under the umbrella of political psychology.

Presentation Formats and Procedure

The conference will feature a variety of presentation formats, including traditional oral presentations and interactive discussion panels.
