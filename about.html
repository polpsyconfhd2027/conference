About The Conference

24 Hours of Political Psychology.

This two-day conference in Heidelberg is dedicated to the theme "Digitalization, Discourse and Democracy - Political Psychology in the modern age."

Purpose

The conference creates space for researchers to discuss how digital environments, public discourse, and democratic processes are shaped by psychological dynamics.

Dates

The event will take place on Thursday, February 11, 2027 and Friday, February 12, 2027.

Conference Theme

We invite work on political communication, digital platforms, democratic resilience, political identity, polarization, public opinion, and discourse in contemporary societies.

Theme

Digitalization, discourse and democracy.

We welcome contributions that examine how people interpret political content online, engage in democratic discourse, form attitudes in mediated environments, and respond to rapid technological and societal change.

The conference title highlights an intensive, compact format with six paper-session blocks, a keynote, and dedicated time for networking and exchange.

Format

Compact and focused.

The program is designed around parallel paper sessions, short breaks, a keynote, and a concluding closing session across 24 hours of conference activity.
